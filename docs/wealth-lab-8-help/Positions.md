## Positions

The Positions visualizer displays a list of all of the trades (Positions) generated by the Strategy and a set of details and performance metrics for each trade:

- Long or Short position, 
- Entry/Exit Price, Date, Signal name
- Profit/Profit %, 
- Bars Held
- Tag

%{color:blue}**Tips!**% 
- **Sort** the list by clicking on a column heading, or double click to zero in on a particular trade on the chart. 
- **Filter** the list by symbol
- To zero in on **open positions**, bring them to top with a button
- To help establish a position in the currently held portfolio of the strategy, you can send all open positions to a new Quotes window. Selecting certain positions will only send them.

**Double-click action**  
Double click a trade to show it in the Chart view.  You can use this action to review trades in the chart after performing a portfolio backtest without re-executing the Strategy on the selected symbol.
